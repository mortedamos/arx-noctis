; =====================================================================================================================
;  SHADOWKEEP  -  EVERY WORD THE GAME SAYS
; =====================================================================================================================
;  This is a plain text file: edit it freely, save, and reload the game.  (No code, no building.)
;
;  A line in [square brackets] starts a SECTION; the lines under it, down to the next bracket, are its words.  Blank lines are ignored, and so is any line that starts with ;
;  If a section is missing, the game uses the words built into it, so you can delete one to put it back.
;
;    [tablet <id>]            a gravestone or tablet (her voice).  Written in mixed case; she has no colons (they turn into dashes).
;    [sign <door>]            a signpost, by the door it points to (hall, mire, chapel, crypts, orloj, clockwork, ice, library, throne, or a room id for THE KEEPER's door)
;    [convo <id>]             a spoken exchange at a gravestone.  One line per speaker:  WHO: what they say.   "HER VOICE" speaks in her own handwriting.
;    [boss <NAME> before]     what is said before a boss fight (once), one line per speaker  WHO: words.   [boss <NAME> after] is said as it dies.   NAME is the name over the boss's health bar.
;    [npc <room>]             a person indoors, by the id of their room:   TITLE: ...   TEXT: what they say the first time   AGAIN: what they say after
;    [villager <kind> street|home|again|shop|title]   the townsfolk (woman, bearded, hatman, oldman): every line is one alternative, picked at random
;    [keeper greet|thanks|poor]                        the Keeper (the merchant in the dark): every line is one alternative
;    [door locked]            {KEY} is replaced by the name of the key        [door sigils]  {LIT} {TOTAL} {HINTS}        [lockhint <boss flag>]  where a boss that still lives is to be found
;    [opening] [ending] [intro] [bell] [lantern] [chase] [shadowlord revive]   the story scenes (see each)
;
;  Most text is in capitals (the game's font); tablets are mixed case.  Keep the lines for boxes short enough to read.
; =====================================================================================================================
