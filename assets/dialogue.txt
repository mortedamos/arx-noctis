; =====================================================================================================================
;  ARX NOCTIS  -  EVERY WORD THE GAME SAYS
; =====================================================================================================================
;  This is a plain text file: edit it freely, save, and reload the game.  (No code, no building.)
;
;  A line in [square brackets] starts a SECTION; the lines under it, down to the next bracket, are its words.  Blank lines are ignored, and so is any line that starts with ;
;  If a section is missing, the game uses the words built into it, so you can delete one to put it back.
;
;    [tablet <id>]            a gravestone or tablet (her voice).  Written in mixed case; she has no colons (they turn into dashes).  A line that starts "The Lady:" (or "Her Voice:") puts that name over the box; without it the box says HER VOICE.
;    [sign <door>]            a signpost, by the door it points to (hall, mire, chapel, crypts, orloj, clockwork, library, throne, or a room id for THE KEEPER's door)
;    [convo <id>]             a spoken exchange at a gravestone.  One line per speaker:  WHO: what they say.   "The Lady" and "HER VOICE" are her: in her own handwriting.
;    [boss <NAME> before]     what is said before a boss fight (once), one line per speaker  WHO: words.   [boss <NAME> after] is said as it dies.   NAME is the name over the boss's health bar.
;    [npc <room>]             a person indoors, by the id of their room:   TITLE: ...   TEXT: what they say the first time   AGAIN: what they say after
;    [villager <kind> street|home|again|shop|title]   the townsfolk (woman, bearded, hatman, oldman): every line is one alternative, picked at random
;    [keeper greet|thanks|poor]                        the Keeper (the merchant in the dark): every line is one alternative
;    [door locked]            {KEY} is replaced by the name of the key        [door sigils]  {LIT} {TOTAL} {HINTS}        [lockhint <boss flag>]  where a boss that still lives is to be found
;    [opening] [ending] [intro] [bell] [lantern] [chase] [shadowlord revive]   the story scenes (see each)
;
;  In [bell], [lantern] and [chase] each line is a box of its own ([chase] is one box) and is said by HER VOICE, in her handwriting.  Start a line with a name and a colon to give it to someone else, as in a [convo]:
;      The Lady: Oh! It will hold me.        The Stranger: I will carry you.
;  Wherever a speaker is named (a [convo], a [boss ...] talk, these scenes) THE LADY and HER VOICE, in any capitals, are her: her handwriting, and the name tag over the box says what you wrote.  Anyone else is drawn as a person speaking.
;  So do not put a colon in the first words of a line that is meant to be plain speech: she has no colons anyway (in her handwriting they turn into dashes).
;
;  What people SAY (talks, villagers, the Keeper, the Lady, the tablets) is written in ordinary sentence case.  The intro, the opening and ending cards, the signs and the door messages are in capitals (the game's font).  Keep the lines for boxes short enough to read.
; =====================================================================================================================

[boss THE RIMEWYRM before]
