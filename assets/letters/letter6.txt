TITLE: The Name I Left
FROM: Wenzel
TO: No one
PLACE: A hill above Prague
DATE: Midsummer 1541

I am writing this on the hill because I cannot write it anywhere else.

The sun did not come up. Something came out of the earth in the night and took it, and the things that follow it came down to the city, and we were on the bridge when the bells began. I had the sword and I was too slow. Zora knew the house and she knew the way, and she ran for her father's door, and a thing with too many arms caught her on the steps.

I held her. She said my name, once, as she had said it every morning for three years. Then she did not say anything.

I buried her here, where she can see the city. I could not cut her name into the stone. My hands would not do it. 

The last light of me that was Wenzel died tonight. He was a soldier who complained about soup, and a husband who kept a window open, and his soul is in this ground with her. What is left will carry the sword, and it will have no name worth saying. I will become a Stranger.

Her family's Work is the thing that did this. I know it as I know my own hand. Whoever sits at the top of that keep has my wife's blood on the dawn, and I will find him, and I will not stop.

I will not stop.
