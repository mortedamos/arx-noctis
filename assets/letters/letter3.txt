TITLE: The Road Back
FROM: Wenzel
TO: Zora
PLACE: A camp on the road to Graz
DATE: Winter 1537

Zora,

I am alive. I tell you this first because I know you will read nothing else until you know it.

I will not write what happened at Gorjani. There was mud to the knee and then there was snow, and the men who led us left in the night with the horses and the pay, and the rest of us were left to the enemy and the winter. The soup I complained of in June I would now give a year of my life for. I have walked from the field to this fire with men whose names I learned and then lost within a day. Do not ask me for those names.

One thing I must tell you, because I cannot make sense of it alone. On the fourth night of the march, in a frozen stream that no one had crossed, I saw a blade standing upright in the ice. Silver, with no frost on it. It was warm in the hand, and no man in the company would claim it. I do not remember choosing to take it. It hums when I sleep. It has a name, though no one told me one. Silberzahn. The silver tooth.

I do not think it came to me by accident.

I am coming home. Keep the window open.

Wenzel
