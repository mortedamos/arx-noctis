TITLE: The Levy
FROM: Wenzel
TO: Zora
PLACE: Prague
DATE: Summer 1537

Zora,

They have a drum, a pike and a boot for me, and the boot does not fit. The King's levy wants every younger son with a name and no land, and I am the youngest son of the poorest name in Bohemia, so here I am. Do not look so grave. I can see you looking grave from here.

It is a war in Hungary against the Turk. Everyone says it will be over before the grapes are in. A summer's walk, a few loud days, and a great deal of bad soup. I intend to complain about the soup in a letter every week, so you will know I am alive by how much I grumble.

Tell your father his compass is safe with me. Tell your mother I ate everything she packed before we reached the gate. Tell yourself I will be home by harvest, and then let me come to the door of your house like a respectable man and ask the question properly, with a clean collar.

Keep the window by the garden open for me.

Wenzel
