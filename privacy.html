ARX NOCTIS

Privacy Policy · last updated 26 November 2026

The short version: ARX NOCTIS does not collect, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the game remembers stays on your own device.

What stays on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game stores the following on your device only (in your browser's storage, or in the storage of the app that shows the game):

  • your saved game (quick saves), including the castle's seed and what you have found;
  • your settings, such as volume, touch controls and display options;
  • on the website only: a copy of the game's files and music, kept so the game starts quickly and can run without a connection (the Android app already contains them).

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing the site data (or the app's storage) in your device settings, or uninstalling the app, removes it.

If you choose Save Game, the game creates a small save file on your device. You decide where it goes and who may have it. We never receive it.

What we receive

Nothing. The game does not send information to us, and we run no servers that collect data from players. The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code, and it does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

The website

If you play in a web browser, the game's files are delivered over the internet by a hosting service (GitHub Pages) at arxn.gameworlds.org. Like any website host, it may process technical information such as your IP address when your device downloads the game, under its own privacy statement. We do not receive those records.

The Android app

The Android app contains the whole game, so it does not download the game from anywhere and does not need an internet connection to play. It shows the game with the web view built into Android. Your saves and settings are kept in the app's own storage on your device. If you have Android's device backup switched on, Android itself may include that storage in your backup on your Google account, under Google's terms; we do not receive it.

When you choose Save Game in the app, Android asks where to put the save file (your Downloads folder, any other folder, or a cloud drive). If you choose Share File instead, the file is passed to Android's share sheet, and you choose where it goes (mail, Drive and so on).

If you bought or installed the app through Google Play, Google handles your purchase, payment details and account under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details. We may see summary statistics that Google Play provides to developers, such as the number of installs or sales by country, which do not identify you.

Children

ARX NOCTIS is a game with fantasy violence and is not aimed at children under 13. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect any from children.

Your choices

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove everything the game keeps on your device at any time by clearing its site data or uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated first, and the date at the top will change.
