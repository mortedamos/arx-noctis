This is my first game.

## AI disclaimer
Arx Noctis is the work of one developer, and AI tools were part of the process.
The music was made with Suno, an AI music generator, for this game.
The fifteen portraits on the castle walls were made by the project owner with Google Gemini.
The game's code was written with the help of an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic), directed and edited by the project owner.
